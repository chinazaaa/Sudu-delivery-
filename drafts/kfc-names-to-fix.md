These are till-system abbreviations that went in with the price list and
were never written out. They are on the live site now, in front of
customers, which matters more than what Google makes of them.

I have guessed at the expansions marked **?**. KFC Nigeria's own menu will
settle them in a minute; please check those before anything is changed.
