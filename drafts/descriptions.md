# Descriptions, drafted for checking

Twenty dishes, not eighty-two. Drinks, sauces, dips and pizza toppings are
left out on purpose: nobody searches for them, and the thirty-four extras
are now out of the sitemap entirely.

Written in the shop's voice: what the thing is, plainly, and nothing a
kitchen has not actually said. Where I would have had to invent an
ingredient I have left it general rather than guess.

**Nothing below is in the database.** Read them, change what is wrong, and
I will write the ones you approve.

---

## KFC

**Zinger burger** — ₦6,699
> A whole chicken fillet in KFC's spiced crumb, in a bun with lettuce and
> mayo. The one most people mean when they say they want KFC.

**Zinger suya burger** — ₦6,699
> The Zinger with suya seasoning through it, which is the version worth
> ordering if you already know what suya tastes like.

**Zinger pepe burger** — ₦6,699
> The Zinger built for pepper. Hotter than the plain one, and not quietly.

**Double zinger burger** — ₦9,799
> Two Zinger fillets in one bun. Ordered by people who have had the single
> one and found it insufficient.

**Kfc double down** — ₦9,399
> No bun at all. The filling sits between two chicken fillets, which is the
> entire idea and the reason it has a name.

**Crspy chkn strip 3 pc / 5 pc / 8 pc** — ₦5,499 / ₦8,099 / ₦11,399
> Crispy chicken strips, three, five or eight. The easiest thing on the KFC
> menu to share, and the easiest to eat walking back to your block.

**Kfc hot wings 3pcs / 5pcs / 8pcs** — ₦5,299 / ₦6,699 / ₦10,599
> KFC's hot wings, by the three, five or eight. Hot in the seasoning rather
> than the temperature, though they arrive hot too.

**Kfc spicy rice** — ₦2,999 (large ₦3,699)
> Spiced rice, KFC's own. A side that most people end up ordering as a meal.

**Yam fries** — ₦2,399 regular, ₦2,599 large, ₦3,199 extra large
> Yam, cut like chips and fried. Not a potato, and better for it.

**Moi moi - reg** — ₦2,999
> Steamed bean pudding, the Nigerian one. Soft, savoury, and the only thing
> on this menu that is not fried.

**StreetBite Burger** — ₦2,399
> KFC's small burger at a small price. The cheapest way onto this menu.
