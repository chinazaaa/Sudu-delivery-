# Care packages and collections: what the market sells, and what to copy

Researched outside Sudu, not inside it. The point of this sheet is what other
people in Nigeria actually sell and what they charge, so we can copy the shape
of it, price it, and sell it, whether or not the contents exist on our site
today.

Caveat worth knowing before you act on any number: the network in this
session can search the web but is blocked from opening the shops' own pages,
so every price below is either a figure a search result stated outright
(marked "found") or my estimate to confirm with your supplier (marked
"confirm"). Nothing here is a number I would let you print without checking.

## The most important finding: they sell by budget, not by basket

Every vendor selling these in Nigeria prices in tiers and fits the contents
to the tier. Nobody publishes a basket and lets the total land where it lands.
What was found, in real listings:

| Tier | What it holds | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ₦5,000 | 10 noodles, 2 sachets salt, 1 spaghetti | found, described as a "5K student combo" |
| ₦25,000 | a package "designed to truly last", contents fitted to the budget | found, a student's own request to a vendor |
| ₦30,000 | resumption and hostel-life basics | found |
| ₦35,500 | 5kg rice, half carton Indomie (22 packs), oats, Milo, milk, tomatoes "and more" | found |

They take orders on WhatsApp and deliver nationwide. Which is to say: the
whole business is what we already do, minus the site, minus the runs, minus
the block we deliver to. We are better placed to sell this than they are.

So Care packages should be a ladder, priced in round numbers, named by who it
is for rather than by what is in it:

- **₦10,000 Starter** — a fortnight of noodles and quick food
- **₦20,000 Month of basics** — the one most people will buy
- **₦35,000 Full month** — matches the ₦35,500 listing above, item for item
- **₦60,000 Term box** — cartons rather than packs, bought once

Parents buy the top two. Students buy the bottom two. The tier is the product
and the contents are the recipe, which also means the contents can move with
the market without the price on the card moving.

## A price worth arguing with your supplier about

Our Local Market shelf lists **Indomie Super Pack, 1 carton at ₦16,000** and
**Indomie Oriental carton at ₦10,500**. The market rate found for a carton of
super pack is **₦9,600 to ₦11,400**, with wholesale from ₦9,350.

If that ₦16,000 is right, a care package built on it prices itself out
against every WhatsApp vendor before we start. If it is wrong, it has been
wrong on the site for a while. Either way it is worth one phone call, because
noodles are the spine of every box on this sheet.

## The other collections, from what the market sells

### Hostel packs, bought once at resumption

Nobody sells this as food. What the packing lists all name, and none of which
we stock:

Bucket and bowl · bedsheet, pillowcase, waterproof mattress protector ·
towel · hangers · pot, pan, plates, bowls, cutlery, cooking spoon, knife ·
kettle, flask · dishwashing liquid and sponge · detergent · bin bags ·
extension socket, bulb, padlock · broom, dustpan, mop · mattress
(₦27,000 found for a 75x30x4 student mattress, ₦25,900 to ₦69,000 across sizes)

Two tiers work here: **₦35,000 Room basics** without bedding, **₦75,000
Everything for a first year** with a mattress and bedding in it. A mattress
is also the one thing on this list nobody can carry on a bus, which makes it
exactly what a delivery shop should sell.

### Restocks, monthly

Same ladder as care packages, one rung lower, and the whole product is that
it repeats. Worth an ordinary calendar reminder rather than anything clever:
the first box sold in September should be offered again in October.

### Study and exam

The market does not really sell this one, which is either a gap or a reason.
Snacks, energy drinks, water and hot chocolate, ₦10,000 and ₦18,000, and we
can build all of it from Market Square and Local Market today.

### Gifts

Lagos gifting vendors sell chocolate boxes from ₦1,700 on the marketplaces,
and the established shops (Gift Box Lagos, Flower and Balloon, Zuzu Delights,
JoyRibbons) bundle assorted chocolates, biscuits, Pringles, imported candy,
sometimes a candle, with same-day delivery across Lagos.

That is the model to copy, and it is cheap: assorted chocolate, biscuits,
crisps, sweets, a card. **₦15,000, ₦25,000, ₦40,000.** We already have cakes
(Celebration Cake 6" at ₦9,500, cupcakes, parfaits), so a birthday box is
one cake plus a sweets tray, and the only thing missing is the chocolate and
crisps.

### Care packages, non-food

Every list naming what students run out of names the same things, and we
stock none of them: toilet roll, sanitary pads, soap, shower gel, toothpaste,
detergent, sponge, first-aid basics. This is the shelf that turns a grocery
box into a care package, and it is also the shelf that repeats every month
without us having to sell anything again.

## Two things that limit what a box can be

**Skincare cannot go in a box.** The builder shows every shelf except
skincare, because skincare rides its own car on its own day and mixing them
would break the run. So a Beauty box is either a link to the skincare shelf,
or skincare learns to ride an ordinary run, which is real work. Your call.

**A box can mix shops, but each shop is another pickup.** Nothing stops a box
holding a cake from Market Square and rice from Local Market. Keep it to one
where you can, two at most.

**Delivery is per box**, set in admin: a run fee and a car-of-its-own fee,
defaulting to ₦4,000 and ₦6,500. A care package can be ₦5,000 and only that
box changes.

## Tailoring and the rest

Tailoring is not a box and should not be one: the price depends on the
garment, so it is a conversation. It lives on Can't find it?, which now names
it along with laundry and dry cleaning, hair and nails, printing and binding,
pharmacy runs, and picking something up from a shop in Lekki. Naming them is
the whole job: "anything" reads as nothing.

Enough of the same request and it stops being a request and becomes a
product, which is how the next collection should get chosen.
