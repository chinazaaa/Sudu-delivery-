## The shelf we do not stock yet

Every box above is food, because food is all the shop sells as products today.
A care package that is only food is half a care package: the things a student
actually runs out of are not on Sudu at all. I checked every shelf: there is
no toilet roll, no soap, no detergent, no pad, no toothpaste, nothing for a
room.

That is one shelf to add, and then Hostel packs and Restocks stop being
grocery boxes and start being what they say they are.

What to stock first, in the order it gets asked for:

**Runs out weekly**
- Toilet roll, single and 4-pack
- Sanitary pads
- Bar soap, shower gel, sponge
- Toothpaste and a brush
- Detergent, sachet and 1kg
- Dishwashing liquid (we have Mama Lemon and Morning Fresh already)
- Bin bags

**Bought once, at the start of term**
- Bucket, bowl, hangers
- Bedsheet, pillowcase, towel
- Pot, pan, plates, cutlery, kettle, flask
- Extension socket, bulb, batteries, padlock
- Mop, broom, dustpan

**Sometimes**
- Insecticide, air freshener, bleach
- Paracetamol and the rest of a small first-aid tin

I could not price these here: the network in this session blocks the shops'
own sites, so anything I wrote down would be a guess dressed up as a number,
and a guess in a price list is worse than a blank. Walk Market Square or your
Local Market supplier with this list, send me the prices, and I will load the
whole shelf in one go.

## Two things worth knowing before you build

**Skincare cannot go in a box.** The box builder shows every shelf except
skincare, because skincare rides its own car on its own day and mixing the
two would break the run. So a Beauty collection cannot be a box of serums
today. It can be a link to the skincare shelf, or skincare can be made to
ride an ordinary run, which is a bigger change. Tell me which you want.

**A box can mix shops, but it costs you.** Nothing stops a box holding a cake
from Market Square and noodles from Local Market: the picker is grouped by
shop and you can take lines from any of them. It is two pickups for one box,
so keep it to one shop where you can, and two at most.

## Tailoring and the like

Tailoring is not a box and should not be one: the price depends on the
garment, so it is a conversation. It belongs on Can't find it?, and that page
now names it, along with laundry and dry cleaning, hair and nails, printing
and binding, pharmacy runs, and picking something up from a shop in Lekki.
Naming them is the whole job: "anything" reads as nothing, and nobody works
out on their own that a hem taken up is a thing you can ask a delivery shop
for.

Enough of the same request and it stops being a request and becomes a
product, which is how the next collection should get chosen.
