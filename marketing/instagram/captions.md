# Instagram captions

Nine posters, in posting order. Post one every two or three days rather
than all nine at once: a feed that fills in an afternoon looks like a
launch and then looks abandoned.

The first line of each caption is what shows before "more", so it carries
the whole post on its own.

The hashtag block at the bottom is the same core set every time, so the
account builds a footprint, with two or three that change with the post.
Put them in the caption rather than the first comment. Instagram counts
both the same now and the caption is where people actually read them.
